Installing OuterSpace in Logic Pro
==================================

1. Unzip OuterSpace-AU.zip. You get OuterSpace.component.

2. Move it into your Components folder:
     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   (In Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the path above.)

3. The build is not notarized by Apple, so clear the download flag once.
   Open Terminal and run:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/OuterSpace.component

4. Open Logic Pro. If OuterSpace doesn't show up, open
   Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

5. Audio FX slot > Audio Units > Levek11 > OuterSpace.

Quick start
-----------
- On your lead vocal: insert OuterSpace and click the switch to SEND. It takes the track's name.
- On the piano / pads / guitars: insert OuterSpace, leave it on CARVE, and pick
  the vocal track's name under LISTEN TO. Start with Amount around 15%.
- Narrow LOW / HIGH on the display to where the clash is (often 200 Hz - 4 kHz).
